01 / WHOAMI

A little about me.

TOGO → MOROCCO

I'm Kossi Richard Allado, originally from Togo and currently completing my engineering degree in Cybersecurity and Artificial Intelligence at ENSA Béni Mellal in Morocco.

What draws me to security is the process of understanding why systems fail: examining attack surfaces, reproducing vulnerabilities in authorized environments, tracing incidents and documenting what the evidence actually shows. My interests center on offensive security and application security, with incident investigation and AI security as complementary areas.

My internships at CMRPI and ONEE, alongside CTFs and personal projects, have helped me turn theory into implementations, tests and reports. I'm looking for a four-month final-year cybersecurity internship starting in February 2027.
